PRE-EXECUTION RESPONSIBILITY LAYER

PRIME-RL Demonstrator

A deterministic, model-agnostic governance layer that separates an autonomous system's capability to act from its authority to execute.
Core rule: If valid responsibility cannot be resolved before execution, execution is not admissible.
